How we work

A simple path from your first message to a marked, mapped plot.

  1. Tell us about your land

    Send the location and what you need on WhatsApp or by phone.

  2. We visit the site

    We measure your plot on the ground with GPS equipment.

  3. Beacons and plans

    Corners are marked with beacons and the survey plan is prepared.

  4. Documents

    We guide you through subdivision or titling paperwork.
